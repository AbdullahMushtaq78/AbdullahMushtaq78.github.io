## Awards and honors

My academic work has been recognized with the following awards, scholarships, and honors.

- **2024** **Dean's Honor List, top 5% of class**, Information Technology University. Specialization GPA 3.83/4.0; Merit Scholarship holder, 2020–2024.
- **2023 – 24** **NGIRI 2023–24 National Research Grant**, IGNITE, Federal ICT R&D Fund. Awarded 100,000 PKR for *Harnessing Mixed Reality and Drones for Agriculture using Generative AI*, selected among outstanding final-year projects across Pakistani universities.
- **2022** **4th place, Google Competitive Programming Competition**, Google Developer Student Club, ITU. Ranked 4th among more than 150 participants from universities across Pakistan.
- **2020 – 24** **HEC Ehsaas fully funded undergraduate scholarship**, Higher Education Commission, Pakistan. A nationally competitive scholarship awarded for academic excellence.

## Media and press coverage

The following coverage relates to Pakistan's first VR classroom field experiment, conducted at [Information Technology University](https://itu.edu.pk/) through Virtuality Labs.

- The research aired live on national TV on [ARY News](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/itu-metaverse-virtualreality-ugcPost-7177955223385108480-05So), covering the VR classroom initiative at ITU Lahore.
- [Samaa TV](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/itu-metaverse-virtualreality-ugcPost-7176867458891345920-4ro_) also covered it live, with a focus on immersive learning with VR.
- [Daily Times](https://dailytimes.com.pk/1179316/itu-lahore-launches-first-virtual-reality-classes/) featured it as *ITU Lahore Launches First Virtual Reality Classes*.
- [Academia Magazine](https://academiamag.com/news/itu-launches-vr-classes-for-immersive-learning/) featured it as *ITU Launches VR Classes for Immersive Learning*.
- The [official ITU YouTube documentary](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S4Pxp9O4fdA) *VR Classes at ITU: 1st Time in Pakistan* covered the project.
- The project was demonstrated live to the [Chief Minister of Punjab](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/ibrahimghaznavi_quest2-vr-xr-activity-7204106284541845504-Xiwn) during an official visit to ITU.
